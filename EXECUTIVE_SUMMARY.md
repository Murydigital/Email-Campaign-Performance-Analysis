
# 📋 Executive Summary — Email Campaign Performance Analysis

**Period:** May – December 2025

**Campaigns Analysed:** 20

**Total Emails Sent:** 947,000

**Total Revenue:** £1,142,300

---

## 1. Overview

This analysis evaluates 20 email marketing campaigns executed between May and December 2025 for a retail brand. The campaigns targeted five audience segments using seven subject line strategies and generated £1,142,300 in total revenue from 947,000 emails sent.

The goal of this project is to demonstrate a full analytics pipeline—Excel, MySQL, Python, and Tableau—and to surface actionable insights to improve future email campaign performance.

## 7. Strategic Recommendations

### 7.1 Expand VIP Programme

* **Why:** VIP Customers deliver £2.59 per email, far above the overall average (£1.21).
* **Action:** Increase VIP‑focused campaigns from **2 to 4–6 per year**, combining Exclusive Offer + Discount Offer subject lines.
* **Impact:** A lift in VIP volume at current efficiency could drive **£50,000–£80,000 additional annual revenue**.

---

### 7.2 Build Pre‑Q4 Engagement Runway

* **Why:** May–August campaigns currently account for only 8% of revenue.
* **Action:** Add **2–3 additional campaigns** in June and July focused on Educational and Product Announcement content to warm segments ahead of peak season.
* **Impact:** Modest performance at current benchmarks could add **10–15%** to annual revenue.

---

### 7.3 Replicate the Black Friday Sequence

* **Why:** The **Preview → Main → Follow‑up** sequence generated **£405,600 (35% of revenue)** from just 3 campaigns.
* **Action:** Build similar **Preview + Main + Follow‑up** flows for other major sales such as the Summer Sale and Halloween.

---

### 7.4 Optimise Subject Line Strategy

* **Why:** Discount Offer and Urgency consistently outperform other types.
* **Action:** Prioritise **Discount Offer** for revenue-driving sends and layer in **Urgency** wording (e.g., “Ends tonight”) to maximise last-minute clicks.

---

### 7.5 Re‑Design Win‑Back Strategy

* **Why:** The Win‑back campaign to Inactive subscribers generated only **£9,800**.
* **Action:** Test multi‑step automated journeys with stronger offers and clearer value propositions rather than single one-off sends.

---

## 8. Conclusion

The email programme is delivering strong results, with high engagement and a clear ability to monetise promotional events—especially during Q4. The largest opportunities now lie in:

* Scaling the **most efficient segment** (VIP Customers)
* Extending the **campaign calendar** into under‑used months
* Systematically applying the **Black Friday sequence model** to other events

By implementing these recommendations, the business can realistically target a **10–20% uplift** in annual email‑driven revenue purely through smarter segmentation and timing.
